Research

I develop methods and open-source tools that give provable guarantees for AI-enabled and autonomous systems, including when their models are learned from data or unknown. My work sits at the intersection of formal methods, machine learning and control, in three connected themes.

1 Verified neural networks 2 Data-driven safety certificates 3 Safe autonomy and robotics

Verified neural networks

Proving how neural networks behave over every input they may see.

Neural networks now sit inside perception, control and decision-making pipelines, yet a small change to an input can change their output. I work on verification methods that prove how a network behaves over a whole set of inputs, not only on the examples it was tested on. Reachability analysis computes every output a network can produce for a given set of inputs, and probabilistic verification gives high-confidence guarantees where exact analysis does not scale [1, 2].

A theme of this work is bringing verification to the architectures used in practice, including vision models, graph neural networks [3] and mixture-of-experts models [4]. I also use symbolic checks to catch and correct errors in the reasoning of AI systems [5].

Tools: NNV, n2v

Data-driven safety certificates

Safety proofs and safe controllers built from data when no model is available.

A barrier certificate is a function that proves a system can never reach an unsafe state, without simulating every possible run. Constructing one normally needs an accurate model of the system, which real systems rarely have. My work builds certificates and safe controllers directly from data, with guarantees on how far they can be trusted: from a single recorded trajectory [1], through data-driven abstractions [2, 3], and with neural network certificates for nonlinear dynamics [4].

Alongside the theory, I lead tools that make these methods usable: PRoTECT constructs barrier certificates for polynomial systems in parallel [5], TRUST synthesises controllers from data, IMPaCT synthesises controllers for large-scale stochastic systems [6], and Scen-Opt gives confidence bounds through the scenario approach.

Tools: PRoTECT, TRUST, IMPaCT, Scen-Opt

Safe autonomy and robotics

Correct-by-construction control and verified decision-making for robots and autonomous systems.

Autonomous systems such as drones and robots must stay safe while acting on learned components. I design correct-by-construction controllers and runtime safeguards, for example a controller that safely takes over when an autonomous system leaves its safe operating region [1], and control barrier functions for systems described by differential-algebraic equations [2]. I also verify the decision logic of robots: BehaVerify checks behaviour trees, a common way to program robot behaviour, against formal specifications [3].

I co-lead the category of the ARCH-COMP competition that evaluates verification tools on neural network control systems [4]. Earlier work applied the same ideas to energy grids and smart buildings [5, 6] and to the resilience of water treatment facilities [7].

Tools: BehaVerify, IMPaCT
